Four pieces that work together

What you need to run your projects, in one place, with changes live.

01 · Tasks

Boards that keep moving

Projects with Kanban boards, labels, due dates, checklists and attachments. Drag with the mouse or the keyboard, and archive what you no longer need.

02 · Documentation

Linked knowledge

Nested Markdown pages with a block editor, tables and images. Link tasks from your documents and search everything without worrying about accents or capitals.

03 · Vault

Passwords only you can read

Logins and notes encrypted in your browser before they ever leave it, with a password generator, two-step verification codes and auto-lock. Import from your browser or Bitwarden.

04 · Agents

Your agents, with clear permissions

An MCP server so Claude and other assistants can read documentation, create tasks and rearrange boards. You see what they do live, and the vault always stays out of their reach.

One copy per person. Your data, at home.

Orqevio isn’t someone else’s cloud service: everyone installs their own copy, and orqevio.com keeps nobody’s data.

Where your copy lives

In your Cloudflare accountA Worker, a SQLite database and a bucket for attachments.
Or on your server, with DockerOne container and a single SQLite file.
Your agents connect to itThrough MCP, with OAuth or a token per workspace.

No accounts, no passwords

No sign-up, no email. You claim your copy once with a setup code and sign in with a random 160-bit key that only you have. If you lose it, you generate a new one from your Cloudflare account or your server.

  • Updating is one command, or merging a pull request.
  • Your copy tells you when there’s a new version.
  • Your data, your key and your vault are always kept.

Security without the fine print

  • Zero-knowledge vault. Argon2id and AES-256-GCM in your browser: the server only stores encrypted data, titles included.
  • Agents with limits. Each connection belongs to one workspace and has permissions per area. Agents can’t delete, only archive.
  • Everything is logged. Every change shows who made it, person or agent, and you can revoke access instantly.
  • Secure by default. Secure cookies, CSRF protection, a strict content security policy and rate limits.

Source available, free to use
