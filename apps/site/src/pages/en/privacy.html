Privacy policy

Your data doesn’t pass through here

orqevio.com keeps nobody’s data. Each copy of Orqevio belongs to its owner and lives in their Cloudflare account or on their server.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no forms. There’s no sign-up or anything to fill in, and we don’t count visits.
  • No third-party resources. Fonts, styles and the copy script are served from orqevio.com.
  • Hosting. The website is served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes technical data of each visit, such as the IP address, to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, under its privacy policy. We don’t turn on visit logs.

Your copy of Orqevio

  • It’s yours. It lives in your Cloudflare account or on your server. HSY Labs has no access to it or to anything you store.
  • No telemetry. Orqevio sends usage data to no one.
  • Your vault is encrypted in your browser. Not even your copy’s server can read it.

Your copy only connects to other sites in these cases:

  • Update notices. At most every 12 hours, and only while you use it, it asks registry.npmjs.org for the latest published version. The request carries none of your data. Turn it off with UPDATE_CHECK=off.
  • Apps you connect with OAuth. When you connect one, such as Claude, your copy reads the description that app publishes on its own site.
  • External images. Images from other sites that you insert in a document are loaded by your browser from where they’re hosted.

The agents you connect, such as Claude, receive what they read in your copy, according to the permissions you give them and their provider’s terms.

When installing and updating

  • npm and GitHub. The installer and the Docker image are downloaded from registry.npmjs.org and ghcr.io, under those services’ policies.
  • Cloudflare. The installer uses Wrangler, Cloudflare’s official tool, with your own session. It turns off Wrangler’s anonymous telemetry, unless you turned it on yourself.

Contact

Orqevio is a project by HSY Labs. For any privacy question, write to us through the GitHub repository.
